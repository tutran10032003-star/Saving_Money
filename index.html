💡 Daily Reminders & Basic Rules:

  • 💡 Turn off the lights: Remember to switch off room and hallway lights when not in use.
  • 🔒 Check the doors: Make sure the doors are locked and the kitchen door is closed.
  • 🔌 Unplug devices: Unplug standby appliances (TV, microwave) when not in use.
  • 🚿 Water heater: Remember to switch off the water heater after showering.
